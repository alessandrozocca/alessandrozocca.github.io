news

Some recent news about my research and activities.

  • Oct 20, 2026
    New preprint! Our paper “Multiplicative Path Weights in Networks: From Katz Connectivity to Mean First-Passage Times”, written with Robbert van der Burg, Bernd Heidergott, and Thao Le, studies network connectivity through multiplicative path weights, linking Katz centrality with first-passage times in Markov chains and extending the framework to random edge weights. Read the preprint on arXiv.
  • Mar 27, 2026
    New preprint! Together with Thao Le, Robbert van der Burg, Bernd Heidergott, and Ines Lindner, we have just finished a new paper titled “Random Walks with Traversal Costs: Variance-Aware Performance Analysis and Network Optimization”. We introduce weighted Markovian graphs, a framework that decouples random walk dynamics from (possibly stochastic) edge traversal costs, and derive closed-form expressions for the mean and variance of weighted first passage times and Kemeny constants. We showcase the framework through two applications to surveillance patrol policies and traffic network optimization under disruptions. Read the preprint on arXiv.

  • Nov 19, 2024
    New preprint on dynamic dimensioning of frequency containment reserves, in collaboration with Jöbke Janssen, Bert Zwart, and Jalal Kazempour. This approach leverages a diffusion model for frequency fluctuations and achieves the same grid-safety level at lower cost. The study focuses on the FCR-N product in the Nordic grid and uses historical grid-frequency data. Read the preprint on arXiv.
