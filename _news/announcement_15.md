<b>New preprint!</b> Our paper <i>"Multiplicative Path Weights in Networks: From Katz Connectivity to Mean First-Passage Times"</i>, written with Robbert van der Burg, Bernd Heidergott, and Thao Le, studies network connectivity through multiplicative path weights, linking Katz centrality with first-passage times in Markov chains and extending the framework to random edge weights. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21474">Read the preprint on arXiv</a>.
