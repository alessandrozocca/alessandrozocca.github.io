<b>New preprint</b> on dynamic dimensioning of frequency containment reserves, in collaboration with Jöbke Janssen, Bert Zwart, and Jalal Kazempour. This approach leverages a diffusion model for frequency fluctuations and achieves <i>the same grid-safety level at lower cost</i>. The study focuses on the FCR-N product in the Nordic grid and uses historical grid-frequency data. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11093">Read the preprint on arXiv</a>.
